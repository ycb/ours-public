# Manual QA notes (Peter, 2026-10-30)

Findings from Peter's first manual pass over the synthetic loop, and what changed. Synthetic data only.

## 1. A rating looked like it needed a message

- **Seen:** after picking a rating, the only button on the idea was **Send**. Pressing it with an empty box showed "Write something first.", so it looked as if a message was required to submit an answer. The rating had in fact already saved, and was already visible to the partner.
- **Also:** the author could not rate an idea until after sharing it.
- **Changed (Claude):**
  - The new-idea form asks **How into it are you?** (optional), saved with the draft or the share, before sharing, so the partner never sees it unrated.
  - The author can rate their own draft on its page. `20261030000002_author_rates_draft.sql` lets `set_item_interest` accept the caller's own draft; the partner still can't, favorites and decisions still wait for sharing, and a draft rating records no activity. `006` was updated from "responses wait for submission" to the new rule.
  - Picking a rating shows a short cue: **Saved · [partner] can see this** on a shared idea, or **Saved** on a draft.
  - **Send** is disabled until the box has text, and the "Write something first." error is gone.

## 2. Agreed ideas were hidden behind "See all"

- **Seen:** agreed ideas leave Ideas, but Plan showed only the latest one under "Just agreed", with the rest behind **See all agreed ideas**. Reviewing and discussing agreed ideas and experiences is ongoing work, so they must be plainly available.

- **Changed (Claude):** Plan is now Spin, then **Agreed · N** with every agreed idea (newest activity first, each with its latest word and unread marker), filtered in place with Refine. "Just agreed" and "See all agreed ideas" are gone, and `/plan/agreed` redirects to Plan with its filters. With filters on and no matches, "Clear filters" is offered once, in the summary line.

## 3. "Not now" becomes Later, part of aligning (decided with Peter)

- **Seen:** "Leave for later" sat on the Spin result, after you'd both already agreed. Later belongs to aligning, and it needs a list of things to come back to and keep talking about.
- **Decided (Peter, 2026-10-30):** Later is its own view in Ideas (**Shared · Later · My drafts**). Its note is optional.
- **Changed (Claude):**
  - **Later…** replaces "Not now…". The dialog asks "Come back to this later?" with an optional "Anything you'd want to talk about first?"; with a note it posts into the chat ("Moved to Later"), without one it posts nothing. `20261030000003_later_note_optional.sql` drops the reason requirement from `decline_item`, and moving an already-Later idea again with no note is a no-op. 006 now covers the no-note path.
  - Later lists every parked idea with its latest word (the note or any later reply), and gets a dot when your partner says something new there. Shared no longer shows parked ideas. Refine loses "Include ideas on hold"; old `?notnow=1` links open Later.
  - The wording is Later everywhere: answers ("They moved it to Later"), labels and chat tags.
  - "Leave for later" is gone from the Spin result (QA 5).

## 4. The Ideas empty state

- **Seen:** "Everything shared is agreed or set aside" is flat, and sends you to Refine.
- **Changed (Claude), then revised in QA 6:** "All caught up. Nothing's waiting on either of you. Share something new, or go plan.", above count pills that are the links: "2 agreed, ready in Plan", "1 in Later", "1 in My drafts" (only counts above zero). There is no separate "Go to Plan" button, since the agreed pill already goes there and New is in the header.

## 5. Spin isn't fun

- **Asked:** research and lean, delightful ideas. Five concepts went on a design canvas (deal a card, sealed envelope, scratch, reel, both hold).
- **Decided (Peter):** build A (deal a card) and B (sealed envelope), with a way to switch between them. E (both hold, revealed on both phones) is interesting but later; it would run over the existing live updates, not iOS tap-to-share, which web apps can't use.
- **Changed (Claude):**
  - Plan has a **Pick from** category and a **Reveal style** switch, **Card** or **Sealed note**, remembered per browser (a look, not data; storage failures are ignored).
  - Card: tap the deck and the top card lifts and turns while the server picks (900 ms); the pick shows when both are done. Sealed note: press and hold the seal (900 ms, with a ring filling); letting go early cancels. Keyboard and switch activation open the note directly, and with reduced motion on there is no deal or hold, and a tap opens the note.
  - The result has one primary action, **Let's do it**, which saves the pick for you both ("It's a date."), and a quiet **Deal another** / **Draw another** link that stores nothing. Leave for later is gone. After saving: Add a memory, and the "another" link takes focus.
  - Stale picks (`OU410`) and archived categories (`OU411`) recover as before; after an unclear save failure the same pick must be retried, not replaced.

## 8. Refine was too complex

- **Seen (Peter):** the Refine sheet (three selects) felt complex, and offered options that matched nothing (every partner × every interest level).
- **Decided (Peter):** one pill row, no sheet.
- **Changed (Claude):**

Only pills that match something in the current view appear, plus whatever is already chosen. Tapping a chosen pill un-chooses it; one **Clear** appears only when something is chosen. The Refine button and sheet, the "N filters on" line, and the duplicate Clear in "Nothing matches" are gone.
  - The interest filter and per-partner favorites are retired; old `interest=` links drop it, and old `favorite=both` / `favorite=<member>` links mean ♥ Favorites (`favorite=1`).
  - On Plan the row ("Pick from") filters both the deck and the agreed list. `20261030000004_draw_favorites.sql` replaces `draw_roulette(uuid)` with `draw_roulette(uuid, boolean default false)` (favorites only); 012 covers it (+7) and the new privilege signature.

## 9. Answers v3 (QA 2026-11-03, real use)

- **Seen (Peter):**
  - The author got stuck when the partner rated an idea low: no way to move it to Later, only Remix or Delete.
  - The partner wants a clear way to decline, not only Later.
  - Moving to Later should be mutual.
  - Creating an idea shouldn't make you permanently "in"; that can change after talking.
  - Recategorizing or retitling an agreed idea shouldn't send it back for a new answer.
- **Decided (Peter):**
  - Both people answer the same way; the author starts In and can change it.
  - Later only when both choose it.
  - Not for us archives the idea.
  - Only a description change asks the other person again.
- **Changed (Claude):**
  - Every member, author included, gets **I'm in / Later… / Not for us…** (at most one primary: I'm in when it isn't already said).
  - **Later is mutual.** One person's Later is a suggestion: the idea stays in Shared as the other person's turn ("They suggest Later" / "You suggested Later"). It moves to Later when both chose it ("You both chose Later"). The dialog says **Suggest Later** or **Move to Later** accordingly, and the chat tag on a Later note is now "· Later".
  - **Not for us** (`pass_item`) works for either person, with an optional note posted into the chat. It archives the idea for both and records `item_passed`.
  - **Edits:** title and category changes save right away and keep both answers. Only a description change asks the other person again, and only that waits behind Undo.
  - **Migrations:** `20261103000000_item_passed_event.sql` and `20261103000001_answers_v3.sql`. The second also sets both answers to Later on any open idea one person had parked, so today's Later ideas stay in Later.
  - **Tests:** 014 (23) is new; 006 and 001 follow the new edit rule and event.

## 10. Email in production

- **Seen (Peter):** no email in production, and it was never verified.
- **Why:** only the local worker existed, and it delivers to Mailpit. Production delivery was
  deliberately out of scope until now.
- **Changed (Claude):**
  - **Shared logic:** the content-free email logic now lives in `supabase/functions/_shared/notify.mjs`, used by both the local worker and production.
  - **Sender:** `supabase/functions/_shared/resend.mjs` sends through Resend, with the outbox id as the idempotency key. Errors come back as short codes only (`resend_rejected`, `resend_unavailable`).
  - **Function:** the `notify` Edge Function only accepts a caller with the cron secret.
  - **Runbook:** `docs/alpha-email.md` covers secrets, deploy, the pg_cron schedule, an optional backlog skip, checks, and turning it off.
  - **Tests:** 6 new (sender and request handling); the worker tests now pass `outboxId`.
- **Peter (decided):** reuse an existing Resend account, with a new domain and a sending-only key restricted to it.

Mockups only, synthetic.
